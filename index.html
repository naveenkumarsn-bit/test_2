Operations Sector Prioritization

Applying targeted reasoning to assess which workflows can be automated, which require human checks, and which remain entirely human-led.

TIER 1 · FULLY AGENTIC

Qualifications Recognition

Using Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers to instantly verify global qualifications against institutional databases, escalating exceptions directly to human officers.

Estimated Savings: ~90% manual effort reduction
TIER 2 · AI-ASSISTED

Scholarships & Support

Multi-agent pipelines gather budget eligibility and criteria. AI synthesizes recommendations, while humans retain final decision and funding authorization.

Human-in-the-loop governance guardrail
TIER 3 · HUMAN-LED

HEI Support & Licensing

Bespoke investor relations and complex audits. AI acts solely as a background copilot retrieving policies and guidelines to assist inspectors.

Policy, quality & investor focus
